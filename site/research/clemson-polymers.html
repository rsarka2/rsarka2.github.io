Dissipative Particle Dynamics of Thermoresponsive Polymers

Simulating how temperature and concentration shape the behavior of thermoresponsive polymers.

  • Jun – Jul 2023
  • Clemson University · Mechanical Engineering

01

Background

Thermoresponsive polymers change their behavior with temperature, which makes them useful as "smart" materials that respond to their environment.

Dissipative particle dynamics (DPD) is a coarse-grained simulation method: instead of modeling every atom, it groups molecules into larger beads. That makes it practical to simulate polymer behavior at larger length and time scales.

02

The Project

I modeled thermoresponsive polymer behavior using DPD simulations on Clemson's supercomputer, varying temperature and concentration to understand how environmental conditions influence polymer dynamics.

03

Methods

  1. ModelSet up DPD simulations of thermoresponsive polymer systems.
  2. Vary conditionsRan simulations across a range of temperatures and concentrations.
  3. AnalyzeCompared polymer dynamics across conditions.
