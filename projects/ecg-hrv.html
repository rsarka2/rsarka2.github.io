ECG Signal Processing & HRV Analysis

Custom MATLAB signal processing to study heart rate variability across rest, exercise and box breathing.

  • 2024
  • MATLAB

01

The Problem

Heart rate variability (HRV), the beat-to-beat variation in the timing between heartbeats, shifts with physiological state. The goal was to measure how it changes across three conditions: at rest, after exercise and during box breathing.

02

My Role

I wrote custom MATLAB signal processing to pull the cardiac cycle features out of raw ECG and quantify the changes between conditions.

03

Approach / Build

  1. RecordThree-lead ECG across resting, post-exercise and box-breathing conditions.
  2. Extract featuresDetect P waves, QRS complexes and T waves in each recording.
  3. QuantifyCompute HRV and compare physiological changes across conditions.

04

Results

The pipeline quantified physiological changes in heart rate variability across the three conditions.
