Truss Structural Analysis & Optimization

Truss designs optimized in MATLAB and checked by hand, built in acrylic, and tested to the highest load capacity in the class.

  • 2025
  • MATLAB
  • Live load testing

01

The Problem

Design a truss with the best possible strength-to-weight performance, then prove it under live load testing.

02

My Role

I designed, simulated and optimized the truss configurations, checked them with hand calculations, and fabricated the final truss in acrylic.

03

Approach / Build

  1. SimulateModeled multiple truss configurations in MATLAB.
  2. Verify by handCalculated member forces and stresses by hand to back up the simulations.
  3. OptimizeTuned the design for strength-to-weight performance.
  4. Fabricate & testBuilt the truss in acrylic and tested it under live load.
