Plastic-Degrading Bacteria Isolation

Isolating bacteria that can metabolize plastic from recycling-center soil, using selective enrichment.

  • 2024
  • Microbiology course

01

The Problem

Plastic persists in the environment because few organisms can break it down. Soil from a recycling center, with long exposure to plastic waste, is a promising place to look for bacteria that can.
