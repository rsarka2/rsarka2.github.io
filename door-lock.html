Biometrically Actuated Door Lock

A microcontroller-driven fingerprint lock that removes physical keys and manual turning for elderly and disabled users.

  • Aug – Dec 2024
  • Team project
  • BU Engineering Design course
[Image: door lock prototype]

01

The Problem

Physical keys and manual turning can make a standard lock hard to use for elderly and disabled users. We set out to remove both by unlocking with a fingerprint.

02

My Role

This was a team project in BU's Engineering Design course.

[Text: your specific responsibilities on the team]

03

Approach / Build

The course runs a full design cycle under real time and budget constraints, set up to emulate working with a client.

  1. Problem definitionDefined the problem and requirements in a client-style process.
  2. CADModeled the lock mechanism and housing.
  3. Circuit prototypingBuilt the microcontroller and fingerprint-sensor circuit.
  4. Iterative testingTested and refined the design across iterations.

04

Results

94%fingerprint validation

A keyless lock that unlocks without any manual turning.
