About

01 · About & Education
About me

Hi! I'm Rohan Sarkar, a third-year Biomedical Engineering student at Boston University in the Kilachand Honors College, concentrating in Nanotechnology with a minor in Chemistry. I'm drawn to drug delivery and nanomedicine, where engineering decisions at the nanoscale translate directly into patient outcomes.

In the Teplensky Lab, I design DNA-based nanostructures to activate anti-tumor immunity and have engineered antibody-targeted nanoparticles for chemotherapy delivery. Outside the lab, I've built clinical AI systems at a Y Combinator startup and led hardware for a hackathon-winning heat stroke prediction wearable. Working across such different settings has given me a wide technical toolkit, but the skill I rely on most is critical thinking: breaking a problem down, testing my assumptions and choosing the right tool for the job, whatever field it's in.
